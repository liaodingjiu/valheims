🌿 Meadows — Starter Biome

The safest biome. Meadows is where every Viking begins their journey. Gentle hills, scattered trees, and passive animals — but danger still lurks at night.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
BoarLowAggressive when approached. Base food source (raw meat, leather scraps). Can be tamed.
DeerLowFlees from players. Trophy used to summon Eikthyr. Drops deer hide and raw meat.
NeckLowSmall lizard-like creatures near water. Drops Neck Tail (grilled for early-game food) and coins.
Greydwarf (night)Low-MediumSpawns at night. Can be dangerous to new players. Drops wood, stone, and Greydwarf Eyes.
Eikthyr (boss)MediumThe first Forsaken boss. Summoned with 2 Deer Trophies at a Mystical Altar.

Key Resources

ResourceUse
Wood / StoneBasic building materials
FlintFlint weapons, arrows, tools — in riverbanks
Leather ScrapsLeather armor, storage chests — from boars
Beech Seeds / DandelionQueens Jam — essential early food
HoneyFrom wild beehives — food and mead base

Boss

Eikthyr — Summoned with 2 Deer Trophies. HP: 500. Weakness: Pierce. Full Eikthyr strategy →

Required Gear

None. You start here with nothing. Craft a stone axe, a bow, and flint arrows. That's all you need.

Progression Milestones

  • Craft a Stone Axe, Stone Hammer, and Workbench
  • Build a small shelter with bed (set spawn point)
  • Craft Leather Armor (full set)
  • Hunt deer for trophies and hide
  • Build a raft (optional, for coastal exploration)
  • Kill Eikthyr → Unlocks Black Forest
Meadows Tip: Find and mark a bee hive on your map. Honey is critical for healing meads throughout the entire game. Use a Queen's Jam + Honey to brew your first Mead Base.

🌲 Black Forest — First Challenge

Dense, dark woodland. The Black Forest introduces Valheim's first real combat challenges: skeletons, trolls, and Greydwarf swarms at night.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
GreydwarfLowCommon enemy. Can throw rocks. Swarm at night. Weak to fire.
Greydwarf BruteMediumLarge, slow, heavy damage. Parry and counter. Drops Ancient Seeds.
Greydwarf ShamanMediumHeals nearby Greydwarves and spawns poison clouds. Priority target. Drops Ancient Seeds.
Skeleton (Burial Chambers)Low-MediumFound in Burial Chambers. Weak to blunt (maces). Drops bones and trophies.
Ghost (Burial Chambers boss)MediumBurial Chamber boss. Drops Skeleton Trophy and Ancient Seeds.
TrollHighMassive blue creature. Two types: log-carrying (large AOE), fist-only (faster). Drops Troll Hide and coins. Can one-shot low-level players.
The Elder (boss)HighSecond Forsaken boss. Summoned with 3 Ancient Seeds.

Key Resources

ResourceUse
Copper OreSmelted into Copper — used for Bronze, tools, weapons
Tin OreFound in riverbanks — combined with Copper to make Bronze
Core WoodFrom Pine trees — upgraded bows, building supports
Fine WoodFrom Birch and Oak trees — finewood bow, portals
Ancient SeedsFrom Brutes/Shaman — used to summon The Elder
Troll HideTroll armor — best armor option before Swamp

Boss

The Elder — Summoned with 3 Ancient Seeds. HP: 2,500. Weakness: Fire. Full Elder strategy →

Required Gear

Minimum: Leather Armor (full set), Finewood Bow, Flint Arrows.

Recommended: Troll Armor (full set), Bronze weapon, Finewood Bow + Fire Arrows.

Key Structures

  • Burial Chambers — Underground dungeons with skeletons and chests. Where you find the Elder's Vegvisir.
  • Abandoned Towers — Stone structures with Greydwarf spawners. Good loot inside.
  • Troll Caves — Dark caves with guaranteed Troll inside. High risk, high reward (Troll Hide, coins).

Progression Milestones

  • Build a Smelter and Charcoal Kiln — start smelting Copper
  • Build a Forge — craft bronze tools and weapons
  • Craft a Bronze Pickaxe, Bronze Axe, Bronze Atgeir/Sword
  • Clear Burial Chambers for Ancient Seeds and Surtling Cores
  • Craft a Portal (requires Fine Wood + Greydwarf Eyes + Surtling Cores)
  • Kill The Elder → Unlocks Swamp (drops Swamp Key for Sunken Crypts)
Black Forest Tip: Build your first real base at the edge of a Black Forest, near the coast. This gives access to both Meadows and Black Forest resources, and a dock for your first boat (Karve).

🌫️ Swamp — The First Wall

Dark, wet, and deadly. The Swamp is the first biome that demands proper preparation — especially poison resistance. This is where many players hit their first major difficulty spike.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
DraugrMediumUndead vikings. Two types: melee (sword/axe) and archer. Drops entrails (food ingredient). Weak to blunt.
Draugr EliteHighLarger Draugr with star icon. Heavy damage, high HP. Two-handed axe. Parry carefully.
Blob / OozerMedium-HighPoison slimes that leave damaging pools. Extremely dangerous without Poison Resistance Mead. Drops Ooze (iron scrap source).
Skeleton (Swamp)Low-MediumSame as Black Forest. Drops bones. Weak to blunt.
SurtlingMediumFire creature that dies in water. Spawns at Fire Geysers. Drops Surtling Cores and Coal.
WraithHighFlying ghost that spawns at night. High damage, bypasses shields partially. Drops chains.
AbominationHighGiant tree-like creature similar to The Elder. Resistant to pierce. Weak to slash and fire. Drops Ancient Bark and Roots.
Bonemass (boss)Very HighThird Forsaken boss. Summoned with 10 Withered Bones. HP: 5,000.

Key Resources

ResourceSourceUse
Scrap IronSunken Crypts — Muddy Scrap PilesSmelted into Iron — Iron weapons, armor, tools, building
Ancient BarkAbomination, Ancient TreesAncient Bark Spear, Root Armor, upgrades
ChainWraith (night), Sunken Crypts chestsMetal beams, crafting upgrades
GuckGuck sacks on ancient treesGuck weapon upgrades, building materials
EntrailsDraugrSausages — one of the best foods for this stage
Withered BonesMuddy Scrap Piles, Sunken CryptsBonemass summoning (10 needed)
Turnip SeedsRandom Swamp ground (yellow flowers)Turnip Stew — best stamina food at this stage

Boss

Bonemass — Summoned with 10 Withered Bones. HP: 5,000. Weakness: Blunt. Full Bonemass strategy →

Required Gear

Mandatory: Poison Resistance Mead. The Wet debuff (permanent in Swamp) + poison damage = death without it.

Weapon: Iron Mace (blunt damage — undead and Bonemass are weak to it).

Armor: Iron Armor (full set) or Root Armor (chest gives poison resistance).

Food: Sausages + Turnip Stew + Deer Stew / Cooked Wolf Skewer.

Key Structures

  • Sunken Crypts — Dungeons requiring the Swamp Key. Contains Muddy Scrap Piles (iron source).
  • Fire Geysers — Surtling spawners. Safe method: dig around the geyser so Surtlings die in water on spawn.
  • Stone Towers — Ruined towers with Draugr spawner. Good for entrails farming.

Progression Milestones

  • Enter Swamp with Swamp Key (from Elder kill)
  • Clear Sunken Crypts — gather Scrap Iron
  • Build an Iron Buckler (parry power increases dramatically)
  • Craft Iron Mace, Iron Axe, Iron Pickaxe
  • Find and farm Turnip Seeds — upgrade Cauldron
  • Kill Bonemass → Unlocks Mountains (drops Wishbone for finding Silver)
Swamp Tip: Build treehouses or platforms in the invincible indestructible trees (the ancient trees with Guck). These are safe from ground mobs and provide a perfect forward base. Always keep Poison Resistance Mead active.

⛰️ Mountains — Above the Clouds

Snowy peaks, frozen lakes, and wolf packs. The Mountains introduce vertical combat and the constant threat of freezing to death. Frost Resistance Mead is non-negotiable.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
WolfMediumFast, aggressive, attacks in packs. Drops Wolf Pelt, Wolf Fang (meat for Wolf Skewer). Can be tamed.
DrakeMedium-HighFlying frost dragons. Hovers out of melee range. Drops Drake Trophy and Freeze Gland (frost arrows).
FenringHighWerewolf-like creature that spawns at night. Fast, aggressive, high damage. Drops Fenring Trophy and Cultist Trophy.
Stone GolemVery HighExtremely resistant to pierce and slash. Weak to blunt (pickaxe or sledgehammer). Drops Crystal or Stone Golem Trophy.
Bat (Caves)LowFound in Frost Caves. Swarms. Drops Bat Trophy.
Cultist (Frost Caves)Medium-HighHumanoid enemies in Frost Caves. Use fire attacks. Drops Cultist Trophy and Megingjord components.
Moder (boss)Very HighFourth Forsaken boss. Frost dragon. Summoned with 3 Dragon Eggs. HP: 7,500.

Key Resources

ResourceSourceUse
Silver OreMined from Silver Veins — located with WishboneSilver weapons (Frostner), Silver Armor, Silver items
ObsidianMountain black rock depositsObsidian Arrows (high-damage arrows)
Freeze GlandDrakeFrost Arrows — effective against Plains bosses
Wolf PeltWolfWolf Armor (fur cape + chest, frost resistance built in)
Dragon EggStone nests on mountain peaksModer summoning (3 needed). Weight: 200 each.
Onion SeedsFrost Caves chestsOnion Soup — high-tier stamina food

Boss

Moder — Summoned with 3 Dragon Eggs. HP: 7,500. Weakness: Pierce. Full Moder strategy →

Required Gear

Mandatory: Frost Resistance Mead. With Wolf Armor chest + cape, you get built-in frost resistance, but mead is still needed as backup.

Weapon: Huntsman Bow + Obsidian/Frost Arrows for ranged. Iron Mace or Iron Sword for melee.

Armor: Iron Armor (minimum) or Silver Armor (best). Wolf Armor chest is recommended for frost resistance.

Key Structures

  • Frost Caves — Mountain dungeon with Cultists, bats, chests. Contains Onion Seeds.
  • Stone Cabins — Ruined structures. Moder's Vegvisir found here.

Progression Milestones

  • Equip Wishbone (from Bonemass) — find Silver veins underground
  • Mine Silver with Iron Pickaxe (or better)
  • Build a Blast Furnace (requires Dragon Tear from Moder)
  • Craft Frostner — the best weapon for Yagluth
  • Craft Wolf Armor / Silver Armor
  • Kill Moder → Unlocks Plains (drops Dragon Tear for Artisan Table)
Mountain Tip: Observe the terrain. Silver veins often have a visible crack in the snow or a slightly different ground texture. But the Wishbone is the reliable way — it pings faster as you get closer. Always carry a pickaxe to break the ground beneath the silver.

🌾 Plains — Open Danger

Golden fields and dangerous camps. The Plains look beautiful but are one of Valheim's deadliest biomes. Deathsquitos, Fuling Berserkers, and the infamous tar pits await.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
DeathsquitoMedium-HighSmall flying mosquito. One of the deadliest enemies per hit. One-shot potential for undergeared players. Drops Needle (for Needle Arrows).
Fuling (regular)MediumGoblin-like humanoids. Found in camps. Drops Fuling Totems. Various weapon types: spears, torches, clubs.
Fuling BerserkerHighLarge, two-star Fuling with massive HP and damage. Slow but hits like a truck. Drops Fuling Totems.
Fuling ShamanMedium-HighCasts shield bubbles and totems. Priority target. Drops Fuling Totems.
LoxHighMassive ox-like creature. High HP and damage. Can be tamed. Drops Lox Meat and Lox Pelt.
Growth (Tar)MediumTar blob that slows and applies tar. Found in tar pits. Drops Tar.
Yagluth (boss)Very HighFifth Forsaken boss. Summoned with 5 Fuling Totems. HP: 10,000.

Key Resources

ResourceSourceUse
Black Metal ScrapFulings (all types)Smelted into Black Metal — best swords, atgeirs, and tools
FlaxFuling Farm plotsProcessed into Linen Thread — Padded Armor
BarleyFuling Farm plotsBarley Flour — food (Lox Meat Pie, Bread) and Fire Resistance Barley Wine
NeedleDeathsquitoNeedle Arrows — highest-damage arrows before Mistlands
TarGrowths (tar pits)Darkwood building materials — decorative building pieces
CloudberriesPlains groundHigh-tier food ingredient

Boss

Yagluth — Summoned with 5 Fuling Totems. HP: 10,000. Weakness: Frost. Full Yagluth strategy →

Required Gear

Minimum: Silver Armor, Huntsman Bow + Needle Arrows, Iron-level food.

Recommended: Padded Armor (crafted with Linen Thread from Flax), Frostner (Silver mace), Lox Meat Pie + Fish Wraps + Blood Pudding.

Key Structures

  • Fuling Villages — Camps with huts, spawners, and farm plots. Primary source of Flax, Barley, and Fuling Totems.
  • Tar Pits — Dark pools with Growths. Harvest Tar for Darkwood building.
  • Stone Hand Altar — Yagluth's boss arena. Large stone hand structure.

Progression Milestones

  • Establish a Plains foothold base (heavily walled)
  • Farm Flax and Barley — process at Artisan Table
  • Craft Padded Armor (Linen Thread-based)
  • Craft Black Metal Sword + Black Metal Atgeir
  • Build a Spinning Wheel + Blast Furnace
  • Brew Fire Resistance Barley Wine
  • Kill Yagluth → Unlocks Mistlands
Plains Tip: Deathsquitos are silent until they attack. Listen for the buzz — it means one is aggroed. A single parry or roll can one-shot them. Always wear at least one piece of Padded Armor to survive a hit. Tame Lox as pack animals and combat pets — they are excellent base guards.

🌁 Mistlands — Fog & Magic

A biome of towering rock spires, thick mist, and ancient secrets. The Mistlands introduce magic as a combat system, with elemental staves and protective barriers.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
SeekerMedium-HighArmored insectoid. Fast, hard-hitting. Drops Seeker Meat (high-tier food) and Carapace. Resistant to pierce.
Seeker SoldierHighLarger Seeker with heavy armor. Attacks from above or charges. Drops Mandible (magic staff component) and Carapace.
Seeker BroodLow-MediumSmall seekers that swarm. Spawn from eggs. Weak but dangerous in numbers.
GjallVery HighFloating tick-like creature that bombards you from above. Drops Tick (magic component). Extremely dangerous — prioritize killing it.
HareLowPassive rabbit. Drops Hare Meat (high-tier food).
Dvergr (neutral)VariableAncient dwarves. Neutral until provoked. Drops Dvergr components if killed. Guard valuable structures.
The Queen (boss)Very HighSixth Forsaken boss. Infested Citadel. HP: 17,500.

Key Resources

ResourceSourceUse
Black MarbleMistlands structures, depositsBuilding material — clean, modern look, high stability
CarapaceSeeker, Seeker SoldierCarapace Armor — Mistlands-tier armor
Soft TissueInfested Mines, SeekersMagical crafting component — staves and upgrades
WispWisp mobs (at night)Wisplight — essential for clearing mist in Mistlands
Royal JellyInfested Mines, Seeker QueensFood ingredient (Seeker Aspic)
Refined EitrProcessed from Soft TissueMagic crafting — staves, magic hood/robe/robe
FeathersCrows, pick-up in MistlandsFeather Cape — prevents fall damage (essential in Mistlands)

Boss

The Queen — Summoned with 3 Seeker Queen Materials at Infested Citadel. HP: 17,500. Weakness: Slash. Full Queen strategy →

Required Gear

Mandatory: Wisplight (crafted from Wisp drop + Refined Eitr). Without it, you are blind in the Mistlands.

Weapon: Mistwalker sword, Krom greatsword, or Elemental Staff for magic build.

Armor: Carapace Armor (full set). Feather Cape (fall damage immunity).

Food: Seeker Aspic + Mushroom Omelette + Plains Salad (Eitr food for magic).

Key Structures

  • Infested Mines — Mistlands dungeons. Contains Black Marble, Soft Tissue, and Royal Jelly.
  • Infested Citadel — The Queen's arena. Massive dungeon end room where the boss fight occurs.
  • Dvergr Settlements — Neutral NPC structures with Dvergr guards. Contain valuable loot but attacking Dvergr is dangerous.

Progression Milestones

  • Craft Wisplight — explore Mistlands without being blind
  • Build a Galdr Table — unlock magic crafting
  • Craft Elemental Staff + Staff of Embers / Staff of Protection
  • Craft Carapace Armor and Feather Cape
  • Clear Infested Mines for resources
  • Kill The Queen → Unlocks Ashlands
Mistlands Tip: The Feather Cape is the single most important item for this biome. The terrain is extremely vertical with long falls between rock pillars. Without fall damage immunity, you will die to gravity more than enemies. For magic builds, carry Eitr meads to regen magic faster.

🌋 Ashlands — Current Endgame

The endgame biome. Ashlands is a volcanic wasteland of fire, ash, and the highest-tier enemies in Valheim. Extreme heat damages players over time, and survival requires the best gear in the game.

Enemies

EnemyThreat LevelNotes
Charred WarriorHighArmored undead with fire weapons. Common enemy. Drops Charred Bones and Fader Fragments.
Charred ArcherHighRanged variant. Fires flaming arrows. Drops Charred Bones.
Charred MageVery HighCasts fire spells and AOE attacks. Priority target. Drops Charred Bones and higher-tier Fader Fragments.
MorgenVery HighMassive fire giant. Extremely high HP and damage. Drops high-tier Ashlands materials.
Fallen ValkyrieExtremeFlying enemy with powerful diving attacks. Raid-level threat. Drops unique Ashlands components.
Ashlands Lava BlobMedium-HighVolcanic blob with fire damage. Leaves damaging paths. Drops Flametal ore.
Fader (boss)ExtremeFinal Forsaken boss. Summoned with 30 Fader Fragments. HP: 25,000+.

Key Resources

ResourceSourceUse
Flametal OreAshlands deposits, lava blobsSmelted into Flametal — highest-tier metal. Weapons, armor, tools
Charred BonesCharred enemiesCrafting component for Ashlands gear
Fader FragmentsCharred enemies, chests, fortressesFader summoning (30 needed)
Ashlands CoreMorgen, Fortress chestsHigh-tier crafting component for legendary items
Volcanic RockAshlands terrainBuilding material with fire aesthetics
Ask & Embla BerriesAshlands groundHigh-tier food ingredients

Boss

Fader — Summoned with 30 Fader Fragments. HP: 25,000+. Weakness: Frost. Full Fader strategy →

Required Gear

Mandatory: Fire Resistance Barley Wine (or Ashlands fire resistance gear). This biome deals constant fire damage over time.

Weapon: Mistwalker (Sword), Himmin Afl (Staff of Frost), or Flametal-tier weapons.

Armor: Flametal Armor or Ashlands Carapace Armor (full set). Feather Cape remains essential.

Food: Ashlands-tier foods — Piquant Pie, Mashed Meat, Spicy Bread. High HP + Stamina.

Key Structures

  • Charred Fortresses — Massive dungeon structures with multiple floors, spawners, and boss-level enemies.
  • Ashland Ruins — Scattered structures with loot and Fader Fragments.

Progression Milestones

  • Sail to Ashlands (requires Ashlands-tier boat or high travel gear)
  • Establish an Ashlands base with fire resistance infrastructure
  • Mine Flametal — craft Flametal weapons and armor
  • Farm Fader Fragments from Charred Fortresses
  • Kill Fader — Current Endgame Completion
Ashlands Tip: The Ashlands has no daylight cycle that matters — the sky is perpetually red and the extreme heat persists 24/7. Build covered bases with fire-resistant materials. Your Karve/Longship will need protection from the volcanic shore hazards. Use siege weapons from the Ashlands update to clear Charred Fortresses from range.

🧊 Deep North — Upcoming Biome

The final planned biome, the Deep North, is an icy polar region at the top of the world. Not yet released as of July 2026, but here is everything we know and how to prepare.

Current Status

The Deep North is the eighth and final biome on Valheim's roadmap. Iron Gate Studio has confirmed it is in development but has not announced a release date. Currently, the biome is accessible in the world generation as a frozen wasteland with sparse content and no boss.

What To Expect (Speculation)

  • Theme: Extreme cold, ice caves, aurora skies. Similar to Mountains but more extreme.
  • Potential Enemies: Ice variants of existing enemies, yeti-like creatures, frost dragons.
  • Potential Resources: New ore type (Frost Metal?), unique building materials.
  • Potential Boss: A new Forsaken boss — possibly a frost giant or ice dragon.
  • Mechanics: Likely requires extreme cold resistance, possibly new mead type.

How to Prepare

Stockpile resources now. Before the Deep North update drops, stockpile: Iron (beams for building), Black Marble (for fortress building), Flametal (for highest-tier weapons), resistance meads ingredients, and all food types. Having a reserve of every resource type means you can jump into the Deep North without grinding earlier biomes.

What We Know

  • The Deep North is procedurally generated like all other biomes
  • It occupies the northernmost region of the Valheim world
  • Current world generation shows ice sheets and frozen terrain
  • No mobs currently spawn there
  • It will likely follow Fader as the next endgame content

🔄 Biome Progression Flowchart

Biome progression follows a linear path, with each biome requiring the previous boss's drop to access key resources.

Valheim Biome Progression Path

Meadows → (Kill Eikthyr) → Black Forest → (Kill Elder) → Swamp → (Kill Bonemass) → Mountains → (Kill Moder) → Plains → (Kill Yagluth) → Mistlands → (Kill Queen) → Ashlands → (Kill Fader) → Deep North (Future)

Key Gate Items:
• Eikthyr → Antler Pickaxe (mines Copper/Tin in Black Forest)
• Elder → Swamp Key (opens Sunken Crypts for Iron)
• Bonemass → Wishbone (finds Silver in Mountains)
• Moder → Dragon Tear (builds Artisan Table, unlocks Plains processing)
• Yagluth → Mistlands access (unlocks Galdr Table, magic system)
• Queen → Ashlands access (unlocks final crafting tier)
• Fader → Deep North access (future content)

❓ Biome FAQ

Common questions about Valheim's biomes.

What is the hardest biome in Valheim?
Ashlands is currently the hardest biome in Valheim. It features extreme heat (fire damage over time), aggressive enemies including Charred Warriors and Morgen, and the final boss Fader with over 25,000 HP. Mistlands is a close second due to the disorienting mist and high-damage Seeker Soldiers.
Can you skip biomes in Valheim?
You can physically sail past biomes and reach any area of the map. However, you cannot gather the key resources needed for progression without killing each biome's boss (which drops the tool/key needed for the next biome). Skipping means fighting severely under-geared.
What biome should I go to after the Black Forest?
The Swamp is the next biome. You need the Swamp Key dropped by The Elder (Black Forest boss) to open Sunken Crypts in the Swamp. These crypts contain the Muddy Scrap Piles that yield Scrap Iron — the foundational resource for the mid-game.
What do I need to survive the Mistlands?
Three things are essential: (1) a Wisplight to clear the fog, (2) Feather Cape to prevent fall damage from the extreme vertical terrain, (3) Carapace-level armor or better. Light sources help with visibility even with the Wisplight. Bring Seeker Aspic for Eitr regen if using magic.
Is the Deep North released yet?
As of July 2026, the Deep North has not been released. It is the final planned biome in Valheim's roadmap. The Ashlands is the current endgame biome. Iron Gate Studio may release the Deep North in a future major update.
Do I need special mead for every biome?
Three biomes require specific resistance meads: Swamp (Poison Resistance Mead is mandatory), Mountains (Frost Resistance Mead is mandatory), and Plains/Ashlands (Fire Resistance Barley Wine is highly recommended). Meadows and Black Forest do not require environmental resistance meads.
What is the easiest biome for building a base?
The Meadows is the safest and most popular for main bases — flat terrain, minimal enemy raids, abundant wood and stone. The Black Forest edges (where Meadows meets Black Forest near the coast) are also excellent, giving access to both early and mid-game resources with a dock.
Which biome has the best building materials?
Black Marble from the Mistlands offers the highest stability and a clean modern aesthetic. Core Wood from the Black Forest is the best early-game structural material. Tar from the Plains enables Darkwood decorative building. Each biome unlocks new building pieces at the workbench.
