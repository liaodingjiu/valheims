🦌 1. Eikthyr — Meadows

The first Forsaken boss. A massive deer-like creature wreathed in lightning. Eikthyr is your introduction to boss combat in Valheim.

Summoning

Item Required: 2 Deer Trophies sacrificed at any Mystical Altar.

Location: Mystical Altars are found in the Meadows biome. Each world spawns with a stone circle near the Sacrificial Stones. Vegvisir (location runestones) are found in Meadows structures like abandoned houses and towers.

Tip: Collect Deer Trophies as soon as you craft a bow. Hunt deer with flint arrows — headshots guarantee a trophy drop more consistently.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)500
Health (2 Players)~750
Health (4 Players)~1,250
Damage TypeLightning / Physical
WeaknessPierce (arrows, spears)
ResistanceNone

Attack Patterns

  1. Antler Charge: Eikthyr lowers its antlers and charges forward in a straight line. Telegraphed by a head-down animation. Easy to dodge by side-stepping.
  2. Lightning Bolt: Eikthyr rears up and summons a lightning bolt from the sky that strikes your current position. A glowing circle appears on the ground before impact — move out of it.
  3. Lightning AOE: Eikthyr slams the ground, creating a ring of lightning around itself. The shockwave expands outward. Run away or time your roll through it.
  4. Headbutt (Melee): A quick forward head swing when you're close. Minimal damage but can stagger.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponFinewood Bow + Flint ArrowsCrude Bow + Flint Arrows
ArmorLeather Tunic (full set)Rag Tunic (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 1-2Level 1
FoodCooked Meat + Queen's Jam + Grilled Neck TailCooked Meat + Berries
MeadNone needed—

Strategy

Eikthyr is a ranged fight. Stay at medium distance and kite with your bow. Shoot the head for extra damage. When Eikthyr charges, side-step and shoot its flank. When it summons lightning bolts, keep moving — never stand still more than 2 seconds.

The terrain around Mystical Altars is usually flat, which helps. Use the altar itself as a pillar to block charges if needed.

Forsaken Power

Eikthyr's Might: Increases jump height by 60% and run speed by 60% for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Extremely useful for exploration, especially before you have a mount. Activate it when gathering resources or exploring new coastlines.

Rewards

  • Hard Antler — Used to craft the Antler Pickaxe, your first pickaxe for mining copper and tin in the Black Forest.
  • Eikthyr Trophy — Decorative mount for your base.
  • Eikthyr Forsaken Power — Unlocked at the Sacrificial Stones.

🌳 2. The Elder — Black Forest

An ancient tree creature awakened by your intrusion into the Black Forest. The Elder is slow but hits hard, with powerful ranged and summoning attacks.

Summoning

Item Required: 3 Ancient Seeds sacrificed at the Elder's Altar.

Location: The Elder's Altar is a massive stone structure in the Black Forest. Vegvisir runestones are found inside Burial Chambers (the dungeon structures with skeletons).

Ancient Seeds: Dropped by Greydwarf Brutes and Shaman. Also found in chests inside Burial Chambers. You need 3 — stock up by clearing a few Burial Chambers.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)2,500
Health (2 Players)~3,750
Health (4 Players)~6,250
Damage TypePhysical / Nature
WeaknessFire (Fire Arrows, Fire Weapons)
ResistancePierce (arrows do less damage than fire)

Attack Patterns

  1. Ground Stomp: The Elder slams its roots into the ground, creating a shockwave in a wide arc. High damage. Roll through the shockwave or hide behind one of the large stone pillars around the arena.
  2. Vine Tentacles: The Elder summons roots from the ground that reach toward you. They deal moderate damage and can stagger. Keep moving to avoid them.
  3. Ranged Thorn Barrage: Fires a volley of thorn projectiles from its body. The projectiles arc slightly — strafe left or right while running.
  4. Summon Greydwarves: The Elder raises its arms and summons 3-5 Greydwarves (including Shaman). Kill them quickly or ignore them while focusing the boss.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponFinewood Bow + Fire ArrowsFinewood Bow + Flint Arrows
Melee OptionBronze Atgeir (if comfortable)Flint Spear
ArmorBronze Armor (full set)Leather Armor (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 2-3Level 1-2
FoodDeer Stew + Carrot Soup + Grilled Neck TailCooked Meat + Mushroom + Raspberry
MeadNone required, but healing mead helps—

Strategy

Fire is essential. Craft at least 100 Fire Arrows before engaging. The Elder is stationary — it does not chase you far from its altar. This enables a pillar-hiding strategy: shoot from behind a stone pillar, then hide when it attacks.

If you prefer melee, wait for the stomp animation, then dash in for 2-3 hits with a Bronze Atgeir or Bronze Sword, then retreat. Watch for the ground vines — they spawn beneath you.

Forsaken Power

The Elder's Power: Increases wood-cutting speed by 60% for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Invaluable for gathering core wood and fine wood for building. Activate before any major logging session.

Rewards

  • Elder Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Swamp Key — Required to open Sunken Crypts in the Swamp biome, where you find Scrap Iron. This is your ticket to the Swamp.

💀 3. Bonemass — Swamp

The putrid remains of a giant ogre, Bonemass oozes poison and summons skeletons. This is widely considered the first major skill-check boss in Valheim.

Summoning

Item Required: 10 Withered Bones sacrificed at Bonemass's Skull Altar.

Location: Bonemass's altar is a giant skull structure in the Swamp. Vegvisir runestones are found inside Sunken Crypts and in stone towers within the Swamp.

Withered Bones: Found in muddy scrap piles inside Sunken Crypts. You'll naturally collect them while mining Scrap Iron. Each crypt has 2-4 on average.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)5,000
Health (2 Players)~7,500
Health (4 Players)~12,500
Damage TypePoison / Blunt
WeaknessBlunt (Maces, Clubs, Sledgehammers)
ResistancePierce, Slash

Attack Patterns

  1. Poison Cloud AOE: Bonemass releases a green poison cloud that lingers for several seconds. Extremely dangerous. The poison deals damage-over-time and can kill you in seconds without poison resistance.
  2. Arm Swipe: A wide melee swing with its massive arm. Covers a 180-degree arc. Block or roll through it.
  3. Poison Blob Throw: Bonemass hurls a glob of poison that splashes on impact, creating a small poison puddle. Avoid the splash zone.
  4. Summon Skeletons: Raises 3-4 skeletons from the ground. They are weak but distracting. Use a sledge secondary to clear them.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponIron Mace (Level 3+)Bronze Mace (Level 3)
SecondaryStagbreaker (AoE clear)Any Poison resist
ArmorIron Armor (full set)Bronze Armor (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 3-4Level 2-3
FoodSausages + Turnip Stew + Cooked Wolf SkewerDeer Stew + Queen's Jam + Grilled Neck
MeadPoison Resistance Mead (MANDATORY)Minor Healing Mead
⚠ Critical: Poison Resistance Mead — Do not fight Bonemass without Poison Resistance Mead. The poison cloud alone can drain 80-100 HP. Craft it at the Cauldron with 1 Queens Jam + 10 Thistle + 10 Honey + 5 Coal.

Strategy

Bonemass is a melee fight. Ranged weapons (bows) are heavily resisted and do almost no damage. Use an upgraded Iron Mace for maximum damage.

The key is positioning: Bonemass sits in water that applies the Wet status (reduces poison resistance slightly and slows stamina regen). Try to kite it to a slightly elevated area with dry ground.

Fight pattern: Approach, land 2-3 mace hits, retreat when you see poison cloud forming. Wait for cloud to dissipate, then re-engage. Use healing mead when below 50 HP. The Stagbreaker is excellent for clearing the summoned skeletons.

Forsaken Power

Bonemass's Power: Grants 50% resistance to all physical damage (blunt, pierce, slash) for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. One of the strongest Forsaken powers — use it for dangerous exploration and other boss fights.

Rewards

  • Bonemass Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Wishbone — Equippable item that pings when near buried treasure, silver ore, and hidden resources. Essential for finding silver in the Mountains.

🐉 4. Moder — Mountains

A frost dragon (drake) queen perched atop the highest mountain peaks. Moder is the first flying boss encounter and requires frost resistance preparation.

Summoning

Item Required: 3 Dragon Eggs placed at the Moder Altar.

Location: The Moder Altar is built into a massive stone structure at the top of the Mountains biome. Vegvisir runestones are found in stone cabins in the Mountains.

Dragon Eggs: Found inside stone nests in the Mountains. Each egg weighs 200 units — you'll need to manage inventory carefully. Frost Resistance Mead is essential when carrying eggs through the Mountains.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)7,500
Health (2 Players)~11,250
Health (4 Players)~18,750
Damage TypeFrost / Physical
WeaknessPierce (Arrows, Spears)
ResistanceFrost (immune)

Attack Patterns

  1. Frost Breath: Moder spews a cone of frost in front of it. Slow wind-up. Deals heavy frost damage and applies Frost status (slows movement). Strafe sideways to avoid.
  2. Ice Shard Barrage: Launches multiple ice shards that track your position. Break line of sight behind rocks or the altar.
  3. Wing Slam (Melee): Moder dives and slams the ground with its wings, creating a shockwave. Roll through the shockwave timing.
  4. Dive Bomb: Moder flies high and dive-bombs your position. A red circle appears on the ground. Sprint out of the area before impact.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponHuntsman Bow + Needle Arrows (or Draugr Fang)Finewood Bow + Obsidian Arrows
Melee OptionIron Atgeir (for when grounded)Iron Sword
ArmorSilver Armor (full set)Iron Armor (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 3-4Level 2-3
FoodWolf Skewer + Eyescream + Turnip StewSausages + Deer Stew + Carrot Soup
MeadFrost Resistance Mead (MANDATORY)Minor Healing Mead
⚠ Frost Resistance Mead is non-negotiable. The Mountain biome alone requires it. During the Moder fight, frost breath can kill you in one hit without resistance. Brew with 10 Bloodbag + 10 Greydwarf Eye + 10 Honey + 5 Coal.

Strategy

Moder is a ranged fight. Craft 150-200 Needle Arrows (from Plains deathquitos — careful) or Obsidian Arrows. Use the altar structure for cover.

When Moder lands, you can close in for melee damage — but be ready to retreat when it winds up frost breath. The key is stamina management. Moder forces you to move constantly (dodging dive bombs and ice shards). Keep high stamina food equipped.

A good strategy: build a small shelter with a campfire near the altar before summoning. This gives you a safe spot to regen health and warm up.

Forsaken Power

Moder's Power: Provides a constant tailwind while sailing for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Extremely useful for long-distance ocean voyages, especially to the Plains and Mistlands.

Rewards

  • Moder Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Dragon Tear — Required to build the Artisan Table, which unlocks the Blast Furnace, Spinning Wheel, and the Plains-tier crafting station.

👹 5. Yagluth — Plains

A massive, undead shaman with twisted antlers and devastating elemental attacks. Yagluth guards the passage to the Mistlands.

Summoning

Item Required: 5 Fuling Totems sacrificed at Yagluth's Altar.

Location: A massive stone hand structure in the Plains biome. Vegvisir runestones are found in Fuling Villages (the stone ring structures with Fuling huts).

Fuling Totems: Dropped by Fuling Shaman and Fuling Berserkers. Farm them in Fuling Villages. You'll get enough by clearing 3-4 villages.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)10,000
Health (2 Players)~15,000
Health (4 Players)~25,000
Damage TypeFire / Elemental
WeaknessFrost (Frostner, Frost Arrows)
ResistanceFire (immune)

Attack Patterns

  1. Meteor Shower: Yagluth raises its arms and calls down a barrage of fire meteors. Multiple impact zones appear on the ground — keep sprinting to avoid them.
  2. Elemental Laser Beam: Fires a beam of energy from its hands that sweeps across the arena. Requires precise timing to roll through or hide behind terrain.
  3. Ground Slam (AOE): Slams the ground with both hands, creating multiple expanding fire rings. Jump over the rings or sprint between them.
  4. Summon Explosions: Random explosions erupt around Yagluth, targeting player positions. These are the most unpredictable attack — stay mobile at all times.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponFrostner (Silver Mace) — DEVASTATINGBlack Metal Sword + Frost Arrows
Ranged OptionHuntsman Bow + Frost ArrowsDraugr Fang + Frost Arrows
ArmorBlack Metal / Padded Iron (full set)Silver Armor (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 3-4Level 2-3
FoodLox Meat Pie + Fish Wraps + Blood PuddingWolf Skewer + Turnip Stew + Bread
MeadFire Resistance Barley Wine (recommended)Major Healing Mead

Strategy

Frost damage is king. Frostner (crafted from Silver) is the best weapon for this fight — it deals bonus frost damage and slows Yagluth. If you don't have Frostner, use Frost Arrows with your best bow.

The arena is surrounded by massive stone fingers of the hand structure. Use them as cover from meteors and the laser beam. The fight is a marathon — pace your stamina, don't get greedy with extra hits, and always have an escape route.

Fire Resistance Barley Wine dramatically reduces fire damage from meteors. Highly recommended.

Forsaken Power

Yagluth's Power: Grants 50% resistance to all elemental damage (fire, frost, lightning, poison) for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Excellent for Mistlands exploration where elemental damage is common.

Rewards

  • Yagluth Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Yagluth's Thing — Required to unlock the Mistlands content, including the Galdr Table for magic crafting.

🐝 6. The Queen — Mistlands

The ruler of the Mistlands, a giant armored insectoid with devastating melee attacks and the ability to spawn seeker brood. The Queen requires magic or high-tier melee to defeat.

Summoning

Item Required: 3 Seeker Queen Materials sacrificed at the Queen's Altar.

Location: The Queen's Altar is inside a massive Infested Citadel dungeon in the Mistlands biome. Vegvisir runestones are found inside Infested Mines and in Dvergr settlements.

Seeker Queen Materials: Dropped by regular Seeker Queens found in Infested Citadels. You'll passively collect enough while exploring these dungeons.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)17,500
Health (2 Players)~26,250
Health (4 Players)~43,750
Damage TypePhysical / Poison
WeaknessSlash (Swords, Atgeirs, Knives)
ResistancePierce (arrows ineffective)

Attack Patterns

  1. Claw Swipe Combo: The Queen performs a rapid 3-hit claw combo. Block the first two and roll the third, or retreat after the first hit. Each hit deals heavy damage.
  2. AOE Scream: Emits a piercing shriek that damages and staggers everything in a large radius. The tell is a visible wind-up — run out of range immediately.
  3. Summon Seeker Brood: The Queen lays eggs that hatch into Seeker Brood (small seekers). Destroy eggs immediately before they hatch, or use AOEs to clear them.
  4. Acid Spit: Launches a glob of acid that leaves a damage pool on the ground. Creates area-denial — don't stand in it.
  5. Teleport: The Queen can teleport short distances to reposition. Be ready for the attack that follows.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponKrom (Greatsword, Level 3+) or Mistwalker (Sword)Black Metal Sword (Level 4)
Magic OptionElemental Staff + Staff of Embers—
ArmorCarapace Armor (full set)Padded Iron Armor (full set)
Armor LevelLevel 3-4Level 3
FoodMushroom Omelette + Seeker Aspic + Plains SaladLox Meat Pie + Fish Wraps + Blood Pudding
MeadMajor Healing Mead + Stamina MeadMedium Healing Mead

Strategy

The Queen fight is a gear check. Your weapons and armor should be max-tier for Mistlands. The arena is inside a Citadel — limited space, no terrain to hide behind.

Focus on destroying eggs immediately when the Queen summons brood. The adds can overwhelm you quickly. Use a Mistwalker sword (which has Mistland's pass-through fog bonus) or the Krom greatsword for heavy slashing damage.

Magic builds shine here: pair an Elemental Staff with the Staff of Embers for consistent ranged damage. Use Eikthyr's power or stamina meads to maintain mobility.

Forsaken Power

The Queen's Power: Summons 3 Seeker Brood to fight for you for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Useful as a distraction in dangerous situations but considered the least impactful of all Forsaken powers.

Rewards

  • Queen Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Queen's Drop (Seeker Queen Trophy / item) — Required to access the Ashlands biome.

🔥 7. Fader — Ashlands (Current Endgame)

The final Forsaken boss. Fader is a massive charred warrior commanding fire and summoning armies of the dead. This is Valheim's hardest boss encounter as of the Ashlands update.

Summoning

Item Required: 30 Fader Fragments sacrificed at Fader's Altar.

Location: Fader's Altar is deep within the Ashlands biome. Vegvisir runestones are found in Charred Fortresses and inside ruins scattered across the Ashlands.

Fader Fragments: Dropped from Charred enemies and found in chests throughout the Ashlands. Requires significant grinding to collect 30. Priorities: clear Charred Fortresses and destroy spawners.

Boss Stats

StatValue
Health (Solo)25,000+
Health (2 Players)~37,500
Health (4 Players)~62,500
Damage TypeFire / Physical / Spirit
WeaknessFrost
ResistanceFire (high resistance)

Attack Patterns

  1. Fire Sword Combo: Fader swings its massive burning sword in a 3-hit combo. Each hit leaves a trail of fire on the ground. Requires precise rolling timing.
  2. Fire AOE Nova: Fader slams the ground and releases a ring of fire that expands outward. Jump over or sprint through gaps.
  3. Meteor Call: Summons a barrage of fire meteors that target random areas. The ground glows before impact — keep moving.
  4. Summon Charred Warriors: Fader raises its sword and summons 4-6 charred warriors. These are high-tier adds — use crowd control immediately.
  5. Phase 2 — Enrage (below 50% HP): Fader's attacks become faster, fire AOE frequency increases, and it gains a new leap + slam attack. The arena becomes increasingly hazardous with lingering fire patches.

Recommended Gear

SlotBest ChoiceMinimum
WeaponMistwalker + Himmin Afl (Staff of Frost)Krom (Greatsword, Level 4)
Magic BuildStaff of Frost + Staff of Protection + Staff of Embers—
ArmorAshland Carapace / Flametal Armor (full set)Carapace Armor (Level 4)
Armor LevelLevel 3-4Level 3
FoodAshlands-tier food (Piquant Pie, Mashed Meat, etc.)Mushroom Omelette + Seeker Aspic
MeadFire Resistance Barley Wine + Major Healing + Stamina MeadMajor Healing + Stamina Mead
Fire Resistance is critical. Fader's fire damage is immense. Fire Resistance Barley Wine is essential. The arena will be covered in fire patches in Phase 2 — constantly reposition.

Strategy

Fader is the ultimate test of everything you've learned. Preparation is everything — bring max-tier gear, the best food, and multiple mead types.

Phase 1 (100-50% HP): Keep mid-range distance. Use frost damage (Staff of Frost or Mistwalker with frost arrows). Sidestep the sword combos and jump over fire rings. Clear adds quickly — they compound the fire hazards.

Phase 2 (below 50% HP): Fader becomes aggressive. The enrage state means shorter windows between attacks. Prioritize survival over damage. Use Bonemass's or Yagluth's Forsaken power defensively. Keep healing meads on hotbar.

Group strategy: have one player dedicated to add control (Staff of Embers / Atgeir), one on sustained damage, and one on healing/support. Communication is key for Phase 2 fire dodging.

Forsaken Power

Fader's Power: Summons a group of Charred Warriors to fight alongside you for 5 minutes. Cooldown: 20 minutes. Excellent for clearing Ashlands and Mistlands content, though the summoned units are less durable than real players.

Rewards

  • Fader Trophy — Base decoration.
  • Fader's Drop (Fader Gem/Core) — Unlocks the final tier of crafting and enables access to endgame Ashlands recipes. Required for max-tier gear.

✅ Pre-Boss Prep Checklist

Follow this checklist before every boss fight for a smooth, reproducible victory.

Gear

  • Best weapon for boss weakness — at least Level 3
  • Best available armor — preferably upgraded
  • 250+ arrows (if ranged fight)
  • Shield (tower or medium, upgraded)
  • Spare weapon in inventory (in case of break)

Consumables

  • 3 high-tier foods (HP + Stamina balanced)
  • 10-20 Healing Mead (at least Major quality)
  • 5-10 Stamina Mead
  • Biome-specific resistance mead (Poison / Frost / Fire)
  • Resurrection totem / body recovery items (if applicable)

Base Setup

  • Portal next to boss arena (named "boss")
  • Small shelter with campfire/bonfire near arena
  • Bed in nearby base (max comfort level)
  • Repair station (forge/workbench) near portal
  • Chest with spare gear and backup weapons

Strategy

  • Forsaken power selected (Bonemass/Eikthyr)
  • Weapon type confirmed vs boss weakness
  • Arena cleared of normal mobs (pre-summon)
  • Full rested bonus (Comfort Level 14+)
  • Daytime start (avoid fighting into night)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Valheim boss order?
The complete Valheim boss order is Eikthyr (Meadows) → The Elder (Black Forest) → Bonemass (Swamp) → Moder (Mountain) → Yagluth (Plains) → The Queen (Mistlands) → Fader (Ashlands). Each boss drops a progression item that unlocks the next tier of resources and crafting. See the complete progression roadmap →
Which biome does each boss belong to?
Eikthyr is in the Meadows, The Elder is in the Black Forest, Bonemass is in the Swamp, Moder is in the Mountains, Yagluth is in the Plains, The Queen is in the Mistlands, and Fader is in the Ashlands. Each biome must be explored in order — you cannot survive in a biome without defeating its boss and crafting the appropriate gear tier. Explore the complete biome guide →
What do I need to summon each boss?
Each Forsaken boss requires a specific offering at its altar: 2 Deer Trophies for Eikthyr, 3 Ancient Seeds for The Elder, 10 Withered Bones for Bonemass, 3 Dragon Eggs for Moder, 5 Fuling Totems for Yagluth, 3 Seeker Soldier Trophies for The Queen, and Charred Fortress drops for Fader. Boss altars are marked on your map when you approach them. Summoning items are NOT consumed if the boss despawns without being defeated.
What is the first boss in Valheim?
Eikthyr is the first Forsaken boss, found in the Meadows. Summon him with 2 Deer Trophies at his altar. Before fighting, craft a full Leather Armor set, a Flint Spear or upgraded Wooden Club, and eat cooked meat with berries for HP. His Forsaken Power reduces stamina cost for running and jumping by 60% — useful throughout the entire game. Full Eikthyr guide →
What gear should I have before each boss?
Eikthyr: Leather Armor + Flint weapons. The Elder: Bronze Armor + Finewood Bow + Fire Arrows. Bonemass: Iron Armor + Iron Mace + Poison Mead. Moder: Wolf Armor + Huntsman Bow + Frost Mead. Yagluth: Padded Armor + Black Metal weapons + Fire Mead. The Queen: Carapace Armor + Magic Staffs + Feather Cape. Fader: Flametal Armor + max-level weapons + Barley Wine. Complete weapon tier list →
Which Valheim boss is the hardest?
Fader in the Ashlands is widely considered the hardest boss — his multi-phase fight demands mastery of every combat system and endgame gear. The Queen in Mistlands is the second hardest, with swarm tactics and a massive health pool that requires extensive preparation. Bonemass is the first major skill check for new players — without poison resistance and blunt weapons, he overwhelms quickly. Fader guide →
