1. Taming Mechanics Overview

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]

All tameable creatures follow the same core taming process:

  1. Build a pen or pit to contain the creature
  2. Drop food inside the pen (species-specific)
  3. Wait for the creature to eat while calm (no red exclamation mark)
  4. Yellow hearts indicate taming progress (hover to see percentage)
  5. 30 minutes of successful taming ticks — typically needs only 3 pieces of food total
  6. You must stay within ~64 meters for the area to remain active
💡 Brew of Animal Whispers: This mead halves taming time to ~15 minutes. Available from the Bog Witch trader.

2. How to Tame Boars (Meadows)

Boars are your first tame. They provide a renewable supply of Raw Meat and Leather Scraps.

AttributeDetails
BiomeMeadows
FoodMushrooms, Raspberries, Blueberries, Carrots, Turnips, Onions
PenRoundpole fence (minimum)
Population Limit5 boars within 10 meters (for breeding)
Offspring Growth50 minutes to adult

Method: Build a small roundpole pen with one open side. Drop mushrooms/berries inside. Lure a boar in by walking near it, then quickly close the gap. Crouch and wait. Keep fire sources FAR away — boars panic near fire and won't eat.

3. How to Tame Wolves (Mountain)

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]

Wolves are aggressive combat companions. Tamed wolves fight enemies for you and can be bred into a powerful wolf army.

AttributeDetails
BiomeMountains (night spawns for starred wolves)
FoodRaw Meat (Boar, Deer, Neck Tail), Raw Fish, Sausages. Wolves will NOT eat wolf meat.
PenStakewall or stone walls (Roundpole won't hold them). Or dig a pit.
Population Limit4 wolves within 10 meters
Offspring Growth50 minutes
⚠️ Starred Wolves: 1-star and 2-star wolves only spawn at NIGHT and try to despawn at dawn. To keep them: start taming at night and stay nearby during the day. Once the first taming tick occurs, the despawn flag is removed permanently. 2-star wolves are the most powerful tamed combat companions in the game.

4. How to Tame Lox (Plains)

Lox are massive, rideable beasts. They hit extremely hard but are worth taming for transport and combat.

AttributeDetails
BiomePlains
FoodCloudberries, Barley, Flax
PenStone walls or dug pit (anything weaker gets destroyed)
Population Limit4 lox within 20 meters
Offspring Growth100 minutes
RideableYes — craft a Lox Saddle to ride

Method: Build a stone pen or deep pit. Toss cloudberries inside. Aggro the lox and lead it in, then seal it off and back away. Lox won't eat if a roof is too close. Sneak-feeding (crouch, drop food, back away) works if you can't build a pen.

5. How to Tame Asksvin (Ashlands)

Asksvin are the Ashlands' mount creatures. Fast, aggressive, and adapted to volcanic terrain.

AttributeDetails

RideableYes — craft an Asksvin Saddle

6. Chickens & Hens (Plains+)

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]

Buy Eggs from Haldor (1,500 coins). Place eggs near a fire source to hatch into Chickens → grow into Hens. Hens lay more eggs. Feed: Dandelions, Barley, seeds.

7. Breeding Mechanics & Star Inheritance

SpeciesFoodPop. LimitGrow Time
BoarMushrooms, berries, carrots5 within 10m50 min
WolfRaw meat, fish4 within 10m50 min
LoxCloudberries, barley, flax4 within 20m100 min

Star Inheritance: Offspring inherit the star rating of the birthing parent. A 2-star mother + 0-star father = 2-star offspring. This makes starred animals extremely valuable for breeding programs. Pink hearts indicate successful mating. Don't overcrowd — exceeding the population limit stops all breeding.

🏰 General Tips: Keep tamed animals behind walls away from raid paths. Tamed animals need food for passive health regen. Use a Butcher Knife to harvest tamed animals without enabling friendly fire. On multiplayer servers, animals continue breeding 24/7.
