1. Haldor — The Merchant (Black Forest)

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]
BiomeBlack Forest
Distance from spawnAt least 1,500 meters
Detection range320-453 meters (bag icon appears on map)
Possible spawn points10 per world (only 1 activates)

Haldor is found in the Black Forest. His bag icon appears on your world map when you get within detection range. Approaching any of the 10 possible spawn locations permanently locks him there — all others are disabled.

2. Hildir — The Quest Giver (Meadows)

BiomeMeadows (usually farther out than Haldor)
Distance from spawn3,000-5,100 meters
Map iconDress icon

Hildir's camp has a map table revealing quest locations — dungeons with minibosses. She also sells cosmetic and decorative items. Like Haldor, she has 10 possible spawn points, each at least 1,000 meters apart.

3. Bog Witch — Enigmatic Trader (Swamp)

Found in the Swamp. Sells unique potions and ingredients including the Brew of Animal Whispers (halves taming time). Her exact spawn mechanics are more mysterious than Haldor and Hildir.

4. How to Find Traders — Methods & Tools

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]
  1. Circle/Orbit Method: Sail in concentric circles around spawn at increasing distances. Haldor is closer to center than The Elder; Hildir is farther out (~1/3 of world radius).
  2. World Map Tool: Use valheim-map.world — enter your seed → check "Traders" → see all exact spawn locations.
  3. Trader Finder: valheim-traders-finder.vercel.app — upload your world.db file for RPG-friendly directions or full map view.
  4. Place a portal near any trader you find — both camps have a protective dome that creatures cannot enter.
