❓ Quick Answers

What order should you play Valheim?
Follow the biome and boss progression order: Meadows (Eikthyr) → Black Forest (The Elder) → Swamp (Bonemass) → Mountain (Moder) → Plains (Yagluth) → Mistlands (The Queen) → Ashlands (Fader). Each biome requires the previous boss's drop to fully access. Complete progression guide →
What boss should I fight first?
Eikthyr in the Meadows is the first boss. You need 2 Deer Trophies to summon him. Before fighting, craft Leather Armor, a Flint Spear or Wooden Club, and eat cooked meat with berries. His Forsaken Power reduces stamina use for running and jumping. Full Eikthyr guide →
What biome comes after the Swamp?
After the Swamp, you enter the Mountain biome. You need the Wishbone from Bonemass (Swamp boss) to find silver veins. Bring frost resistance mead or Wolf Armor — the Mountain's freezing effect will kill you otherwise. Complete biome guide →
What are the best weapons in Valheim?
It depends on your biome and enemy type. Maces excel in the Swamp (Bonemass is weak to blunt). Atgeirs provide the best crowd control for Plains. Bows are essential for safe dragon fights. Magic staffs dominate in Mistlands and Ashlands. Full weapon tier list →
