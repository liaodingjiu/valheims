1. How Cooking Works

[ AdSense Ad Unit — Responsive Rectangle ]

You can eat three different foods at once. Each provides health, stamina, and/or Eitr (for magic). Food color coding: Red fork = Health-focused, Yellow fork = Stamina-focused, Blue fork = Eitr-focused, White fork = Balanced. Higher-tier foods last longer and provide stronger buffs.
