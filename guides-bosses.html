Valheim Boss Guides — All 7 Forsaken Bosses

Every Forsaken boss from Meadows to Ashlands, covered in exhaustive detail. Each guide includes summoning requirements, attack patterns, weaknesses and resistances, best weapons, step-by-step strategy, and the Forsaken power reward. Whether you're facing your first Eikthyr or the final Fader. See the complete Valheim progression order → We've got you covered.

🗡️ First time? Start here.

Not sure which boss to tackle first? The Valheim boss progression is linear — each boss drops an item required to access the next biome's resources.

All Boss Guides

  • 🦌 Eikthyr — 1st Boss (Meadows)

    Lightning stag. Summon with 2 Deer Trophies. Weak to Pierce. Forsaken Power: 60% reduced run/jump stamina.

    Read Guide

  • 🌳 The Elder — 2nd Boss (Black Forest)

    Ancient tree entity. Summon with 3 Ancient Seeds. Weak to Fire. Forsaken Power: 60% faster wood/ore chopping.

    Read Guide

  • ☠️ Bonemass — 3rd Boss (Swamp)

    Poisonous blob of bones. Summon with 10 Withered Bones. Weak to Blunt. Forsaken Power: Massive physical resistance.

    Read Guide

  • 🐉 Moder — 4th Boss (Mountains)

    Frost-breathing dragon. Summon with 3 Dragon Eggs. Weak to Fire. Forsaken Power: Always tailwind while sailing.

    Read Guide

  • 👑 Yagluth — 5th Boss (Plains)

    Goblin king, fire magic. Summon with 5 Fuling Totems. Weak to Spirit. Forsaken Power: Elemental resistance.

    Read Guide

  • 🐛 The Queen — 6th Boss (Mistlands)

    Seeker matriarch, swarm tactics. Summon with 3 Seeker Soldier Trophies. Forsaken Power: Massive mining speed.

    Read Guide

  • 💀 Fader — 7th Boss (Ashlands)

    Skeletal flame warlord. Summon via Charred Fortress drops. Forsaken Power: Combined offense + defense buff.

    Read Guide
