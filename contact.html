Contact Us

Have a question, found an error, or want to collaborate? We'd love to hear from you.

Get in Touch

If you spot a mistake in one of our guides, have a suggestion for new content, or want to discuss advertising or collaboration opportunities, please reach out.

📧 Contact Emails

Technical Support: support@valheims.com — site issues, broken links, bug reports

Author / Content: alex@valheims.com — content corrections, guide suggestions, advertising, partnerships

Response Time

We aim to respond to all emails within 48 hours. During game update weeks, response times may be slightly longer as we prioritize updating guides.

Before You Email

  • If you're asking a gameplay question, check our Beginner Guide or other guides — your answer may already be there!
  • For bugs or technical issues with Valheim, please use the official Valheim support — we're just a fan guide site.
  • For mod-related questions, check the mod's page on Thunderstore.
