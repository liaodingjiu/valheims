❓ Quick Answers

I just started Valheim. What should I do first?
Focus on three basics: gather wood, stone, and deer/boar hides. Build a small shelter with a bed and campfire for the Rested buff. Place and upgrade a Workbench — this unlocks core crafting recipes and gives you a safe respawn point. Your first day is about survival, not speed. Complete beginner walkthrough →
Where should I build my first real base?
Start in the Meadows near water and close to a Black Forest border for easy metal access later. Flat terrain gives you room to expand. Build near the coast for boat access. You can have multiple specialized bases as you progress — a main home, mining outposts, farms, and harbors. Best base locations guide →
When am I ready to fight Eikthyr?
You are ready when you have a full Leather Armor set, a weapon upgraded at least once (Flint Spear or Wooden Club), and a solid food combo of cooked meat + grilled neck tail + berries. Place a campfire and bed near his altar so you can re-buff and recover quickly if you die. Full Eikthyr guide →
What should I do after beating Eikthyr?
Eikthyr drops the Antler Pickaxe, which unlocks mining. Your next step is moving into the Black Forest to gather copper and tin, smelt bronze at a Forge, and upgrade your gear. This marks the transition from basic survival into real progression — the game opens up dramatically from here. Follow the complete progression roadmap →
What are the best weapons in Valheim?
It depends on your biome. Maces dominate the Swamp (Bonemass is weak to blunt). Atgeirs provide the best crowd control for Plains. Bows are essential for dragon fights. Magic staffs dominate in Mistlands and Ashlands. Match your weapon type to the enemies you are facing — damage types matter enormously. Full weapon tier list →
What are common beginner mistakes to avoid?
The biggest traps: pushing into harder biomes with weak armor and bad food, ignoring the Rested buff (+50% HP regen), forgetting you can repair tools for free at the Workbench, carrying too much loot and dying far from base, and sprinting into fights with no stamina. Preparation beats courage every time. Tips for surviving your first days →
